Who we are

Differentiated value proposition in project management

  • Reduction of administrative and coordination burden.
  • Faster response capability for the client organisation without adapting to variable needs.
  • Savings in training costs for staff that may be temporary.
  • Lower risks borne in contracting and peak situations.

Differentiated value proposition in engineering

  • Tailored engineering solutions.
  • End-to-end approach: from concept to commissioning.
  • Data-driven engineering: models, digital simulation, BIM, digital twins and data analytics.
  • Technical rigour and regulatory compliance (IEC, IEEE, PMI, ISO).
  • Continuous innovation and technology updates (AI, digitalisation, industrial automation, renewables).
  • High reliability and reduced project risks.

Vision

Fusgement aims to become a recognised strategic partner for integrated project management, enabling organizations to deliver initiatives with greater effectiveness, efficiency and sustainability. Through a specialised project management office and a strong engineering and consulting foundation, the company seeks to drive better-informed decisions, coordinate efforts and optimise resources.

Mission

Our mission is to provide project management services to national and international companies and deliver quality engineering and consulting services.

Values "PERICIA"

The values that guide our activity reflect how we understand work, professional relationships, and the contribution we want to make to the sector and society. These values are aligned with current regulations, the Sustainable Development Goals, and ESG principles of corporate governance. Interestingly, all of them, together, perfectly define our way of working: PERICIA, the spanish word for expertise.

  • Perseverance

    Consistency and tenacity until the objectives are achieved.

  • Efficiency and Sustainability

    Promoting the efficient use of resources and energy, with a sustainable and responsible approach.

  • Resilience

    Ability to adapt and find solutions even in the face of complex and difficult problems.

  • Information

    Having clear and accessible information to guarantee control and transparency at each stage of the project.

  • Curiosity

    Constant interest in Explore, understand, and improve how things work.

  • Inclusion

    Fostering diverse perspectives to create more comprehensive and effective collaborative environments.

  • Always Learning

    Learning from experience and documenting lessons for continuous improvement.

Founder

Alberto Trujillo Sanz

Registered Professional Electrical Engineer with experience in project management and direction of engineering projects, from simple technical initiatives to complex projects with multiple stakeholders and budgets exceeding €1 million. Specialized in ensuring on-time project execution, coordinating technical teams, and simultaneously managing multiple initiatives. He has worked in industrial, energy, and consulting environments, participating in projects for top-tier clients such as Iberdrola, Schneider Electric, Naturgy, Google, McDonald’s, and Technip Energies. He has experience in projects related to electrical substations, protection systems, energy storage, and UPS solutions, contributing a solid technical perspective to the development and monitoring of the various project phases. His approach combines technical knowledge and project management, adapting the methodology to each client and situation to guarantee results. He stands out for his coordination skills, decision-making abilities, and agile response to changes, providing efficient and execution-oriented solutions.
