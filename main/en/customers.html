Our clients

We work with:

  • EPCs that need engineering and project management support.

    We support installers and EPC companies that need help with electrical engineering and project management to handle a larger volume of projects, coordinate suppliers, meet execution deadlines or strengthen their technical capabilities during high-workload periods.

  • Large consultancies and engineering firms that subcontract packages.

    We collaborate with engineering and consulting firms that outsource work packages, need independent technical reviews or require specialists for projects with demanding technical and documentary requirements.

  • Utilities and ESCOs that outsource project management.

    We work with utilities and energy service companies that develop electrification, energy efficiency, renewables or facility modernisation projects, providing both technical and project management support.

  • Renewable energy developers.

    We support renewable plant developers throughout the different project phases, from engineering to technical coordination, execution follow-up and acceptance testing.

  • Energy-intensive industries seeking efficiency and automation, electrification or heat-pump projects.

    We collaborate with industrial companies that want to develop automation, electrification, energy efficiency, heat pump deployment or facility improvement projects.

  • Public administrations and municipalities.

    We support public bodies and organisations developing tenders or projects related to infrastructure, energy efficiency, electrical networks or facility modernisation.

  • Other organisations and individuals interested in our services.

    We also collaborate with any organisation that needs specialised support in project management, electrical engineering or technical assistance for complex projects.

If your organisation falls into one of these categories and needs support in project management, electrical engineering or technical consulting, please get in touch.
