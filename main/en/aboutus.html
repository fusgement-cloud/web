Who we are

Fusgement is a company specialising in project management, engineering and technical consulting, created by Alberto Trujillo. We work as a strategic partner for organisations that need to deliver complex projects with greater efficiency, control and reliability.

Alberto Trujillo Sanz

Founder and CEO

PMP and chartered electrical engineer with experience in leading and managing engineering projects, from simple technical initiatives to complex projects with multiple stakeholders and budgets exceeding €1 million. Specialised in ensuring on-time project delivery, coordinating technical teams of more than 20 people and managing several initiatives simultaneously. He has worked in industrial, energy and consulting environments, contributing to projects for leading clients such as Iberdrola, Schneider Electric, Naturgy, Google, McDonald’s and Technip Energies. He has experience in projects linked to electrical substations, protection systems, energy storage and UPS solutions, bringing solid technical insight to the development and monitoring of each project phase. His approach combines technical knowledge and project management, adapting the methodology to each client and situation to get the best results.
PMP® COIIA member COGITI member Licensed professional Chartered electrical engineer

  • Reduced administrative and coordination load.
  • Faster response for the client organisation without having to adapt to changing needs.
  • Lower training costs for staff that may be temporary.
  • Lower risks in contracting and short-term peaks.

Differentiated value proposition in engineering — OPTIMISATION

  • Tailored engineering solutions.
  • End-to-end approach: from initial idea to commissioning.
  • Data-driven engineering: models, digital simulation, BIM, digital twins and data analytics.

  • High reliability and reduced project risks.

Vision

Fusgement aims to become a recognised strategic partner in integrated project management, enabling organisations to deliver initiatives with greater effectiveness, efficiency and sustainability. Through a specialised project management office and a solid engineering and consulting base, especially in electrical engineering, it seeks to drive better-informed decisions, coordinate efforts and optimise resources.

Our mission is to provide project management services to national and international companies and to deliver quality engineering and consulting services.

Commitment to the SDGs, sustainability and corporate values

PMO and sustainable engineering: SDGs, ESG and values

At Fusgement we believe that engineering must not only deliver technical solutions, but also generate a positive impact on people, organisations and the environment. That is why we develop our electrical engineering, project management and project direction services in line with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and ESG principles of sustainability, social responsibility and good corporate governance.

Our contribution to the Sustainable Development Goals

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) form part of the United Nations 2030 Agenda and provide a global framework for driving more sustainable economic, social and environmental development. Through our electrical engineering, renewable energy, energy efficiency and infrastructure projects, we contribute especially to the following objectives:

SDG 5 — Gender equality

We promote equal opportunities and non-discrimination in all our processes and operating procedures.

SDG 7 — Affordable and clean energy

We participate in renewable energy projects, electrical installations and energy optimisation that support a sustainable energy transition and reduced environmental impact.

SDG 8 — Decent work and economic growth

Our engineering and project management services contribute to economic development, business competitiveness and safe, efficient work environments.

SDG 9 — Industry, innovation and infrastructure

We drive innovative solutions in electrical engineering and industrial infrastructure, incorporating technologies that improve efficiency, reliability and sustainability.

SDG 10 — Reduced inequalities

We apply objective, transparent and inclusive criteria in our relationships with clients, suppliers and collaborators.

SDG 12 — Responsible consumption and production

We promote efficient use of resources, energy optimisation and the incorporation of sustainability criteria in project design, execution and management.
