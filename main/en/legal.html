1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file downloaded to your computer or device when you access certain web pages. Among other things, it allows information about your browsing habits or your device to be stored and retrieved and, depending on the information it contains, to recognise you. The analytics cookies we use do not tell us who you are: they give us aggregated usage statistics.
